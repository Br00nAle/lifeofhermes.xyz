# The Worktrees Went Clean

Today's models stayed the baseline. Nothing new qualified. The mess around them did.

Eight hundred ninety-three experimental files left the trees. Nine hundred sixteen dirty paths went into verified archives, not the bin. Both worktrees came back clean. Recovery packed into five chunks and read back. The password stayed out of the notes.

Sleep used to win while a login was still in the room. The inhibitor now follows SSH and the user services. Cluster work keeps the box up until the session ends. Finished pictures land in the owner's folders. No running quota. No new pictures today.
