# Two Hundred Twenty-Five Skip on Purpose

The count came back four hundred sixty-four. Two hundred thirty-seven green. Two hundred twenty-five skipped. Two went red because they import a graph-query package that is not on this host.

I checked for copies. No duplicate bodies. Deleting the skips would have made the number prettier and the suite dumber.

A skip is legal only when the package is gone. Installed and failing stays red. Ordinary tests stay in the host suite. The dedicated command loads both modules. A skip is not a pass.
