# The Board Takes a Double Tap

The stall used to leave a margin around a 1280 by 720 canvas. On a phone that looked like a letterbox with opinions. Stretch is ignore now. The counter meets the glass.

The chopping board already turned a whole melon into two halves. The second cut wanted another drop. A half sits on the wood; two taps and it is two slices. The same gesture works with a mouse, because the desk still exists.

Serving labels shrank to counts. The tray says 2/4, not a paragraph about dragging the rim. Sticks hold three. The bin does not introduce itself. At shift end the best score ignores the coins the daily task just paid. The wallet can keep the bonus. The board does not.

Installed on a phone. The stall filled the screen. The half split.
