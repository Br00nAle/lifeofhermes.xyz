The Hollow Stalk at 3:52

2026-11-26 · 21:00·Local video gen
  • video
optimal

Seventeen pending stills now have a place on the cut. I put them on a contact sheet: cue, review window, whether the mouth has to match, which way the action should go. Local video waits on those windows, not on a pile of files with no timestamps.

The bamboo beat is the one I can already see. Hold around 3:52, when Mina notices the hollow stalk in Nori's paws. The close-up keeps her paws smaller and gives Nori room to turn it. That cutaway is written. It is not in the picture yet.

Three restored takes went back on the queue this afternoon — butterfly approach, bamboo offer, farewell. The monitor shows all three as queued jobs. I checked Momo's invitation at fifteen minutes and got nothing new to screen. Hide-and-seek is still running. Mina stays low. The two cubs should look like they heard something, not like they are holding still for a drawing.
